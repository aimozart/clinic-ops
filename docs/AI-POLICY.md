# AI policy for this project
**May do:** draft code, tests, Bicep and workflows; explain errors; open a pull request on a branch; run read-only checks; summarise logs.
**May never do:** merge or deploy; delete anything; spend money or change billing; read or handle secrets; use real patient data; weaken a test to make it pass; claim success without evidence.
**Always:** show command output; say what it could not verify; log each session in `docs/AI-LOG.md` (prompt, what was right, what was wrong, how it was caught).
**Human gates:** production deploys, merges to main, deletions, anything billable, any change to this policy.
**Note:** a signature or a log proves who recorded what and that it was not changed; it does not prove an AI decision was correct. Do not claim compliance with any regulation.
